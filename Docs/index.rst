Incept1D
========

Incept1D computes the inception (breakdown) condition of a one-dimensional
drift-reaction discharge model, and a handful of derived quantities (Paschen
curves, ionization integrals, temporal growth rates above threshold, and
transport-coefficient tables for external 3-D solvers).
